KSERS 2027 Terms of Use and Privacy Policy

Part 1. Terms of Use

Article 1 (Purpose)

These Terms set out the conditions and procedures for using the KSERS 2027 online services (the "Services") provided by the Korean Society of Endo-Laparoscopic & Robotic Surgery (the "Society"), and the rights and obligations of the Society and its Users.

Article 2 (Definitions)

  1. 1. "Services" means the online services provided on the KSERS 2027 website (ksers.kr) and the account and registration system (reg.ksers.kr), including account sign-up, abstract submission, congress registration and fee payment, and congress information.
  2. 2. "User" means a person who agrees to these Terms, enters personal information and creates an account.
  3. 3. "ID" means the e-mail address used to identify the User and to log in.
  4. 4. "Secretariat" means the KSERS 2027 Congress Secretariat, which operates the Services on behalf of the Society.

Article 3 (Effect and Amendment of the Terms)

  1. 1. These Terms take effect when posted on the website and agreed to by the User.
  2. 2. The Society may amend these Terms within the scope of applicable laws. Amendments and their effective date will be announced on the website at least 7 days in advance.

Article 4 (Sign-up and Accounts)

  1. 1. Sign-up is completed when the User enters the required information and agrees to these Terms and to the collection and use of personal information.
  2. 2. Accounts are operated separately for each congress year. A KSERS 2027 account can be used only for KSERS 2027 and is not linked to accounts of previous or future congresses.
  3. 3. Persons under the age of 14 may not create an account.
  4. 4. The Society may refuse a sign-up or delete an account if the User uses another person's information or false information, omits required information, or seriously disrupts the operation of the Services.

Article 5 (Changes to Account Information)

  1. 1. Users may update their information on My Page. However, the ID (e-mail) and country cannot be changed after sign-up; please contact the Secretariat if a change is needed.
  2. 2. The name (Korean and English) used on the name badge and certificate cannot be changed on My Page after the registration fee has been paid (or, for participants exempt from the fee, after registration has been completed). Any change after that must be made through the Secretariat.
  3. 3. Passwords can be changed after confirming the current password. If you forget your password, you can reset it through a link sent to your registered e-mail address.
  4. 4. The Society is not responsible for any disadvantage caused by the User's failure to keep information up to date.

Article 6 (Services Provided)

The Society provides the following Services to Users.

  1. 1. Abstract submission and notification of review results
  2. 2. Congress registration, fee payment and receipts
  3. 3. Support for CME credit applications (eligible participants only)
  4. 4. Congress schedule, program and other information
  5. 5. Other services needed to run the congress

Article 7 (Registration Fees, Cancellation and Refunds)

  1. 1. Registration fees, periods, payment methods and fee exemptions follow the registration guide on the website. Domestic participants pay in Korean won (KRW) and overseas participants in US dollars (USD).
  2. 2. Cancellations are accepted only when requested in writing by e-mail to the Secretariat (ksers2027@ksers.kr). Cancellations requested by March 31 (Wed), 2027 receive a full refund; no refunds are given from April 1 (Thu), 2027. Refunds are processed within one month after the congress, and all bank charges for the transfer are borne by the registrant.
  3. 3. Refunds are processed for the full registration fee only. Partial cancellation is not available.
  4. 4. If the User enters inaccurate information used to calculate the fee, such as membership status or category, the Society may charge the difference or cancel the registration.

Article 8 (Abstract Submission)

  1. 1. The submitter and authors are responsible for the content of the abstract.
  1. 2. The submitter must obtain the consent of co-authors before entering their information.
  1. 3. Accepted abstracts may be published in the program book, abstract book and website.
  1. 4. Submitters of video presentations must remove all patient-identifiable information from the video and obtain the necessary consent and rights before submission.
  2. 5. Submitted videos may be used for abstract review and for presentation and screening during KSERS 2027.

Article 9 (User Obligations)

  1. 1. Users must enter accurate information about themselves.
  2. 2. Users are responsible for managing their ID and password and must not let others use them.
  3. 3. Users must not infringe the rights of others or disrupt the Services.

Article 10 (Suspension of Services)

The Society may suspend the Services temporarily for maintenance, system failure, natural disasters or other unavoidable reasons, with advance notice where possible.

Article 11 (Account Closure and Deletion of Information)

  1. 1. There is no separate account withdrawal function. Users may request deletion of their information at any time under Part 2, Article 7.
  2. 2. If a User who has paid the registration fee requests deletion, the Secretariat will process the request after checking the registration and payment status.
  3. 3. All accounts are deleted after the congress in accordance with Part 2, Article 4.

Article 12 (Limitation of Liability)

The Society is not liable for damages caused by natural disasters, the User's fault, or the intentional act or negligence of third parties, except in cases of the Society's willful misconduct or gross negligence.

Article 13 (Governing Law and Jurisdiction)

These Terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea. Any dispute related to the Services shall be submitted to the Seoul Central District Court.
